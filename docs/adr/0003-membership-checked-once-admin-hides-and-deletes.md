# Membership is checked once at signup; the Admin hides and deletes Members by hand

A Member's Discord account must pass the TOLC membership check once, at signup, and again only if they connect a different Discord account. After that the app never asks Discord again. Instead the Admin hides a Member (keeping their profile, so the Admin can reactivate them later without them redoing it) or deletes them, usually right after removing them from the TOLC server. We considered rechecking at every sign-in and daily, plus a scheduled bot sweep for Members who never sign in again (#16 as first written). We rejected both: almost every departure is the Admin removing someone themselves, and a sweep would need a Discord bot, a bot token, and a scheduler to maintain. The accepted gap is that a Member who leaves TOLC on their own stays in the Directory until the Admin notices and hides or deletes them.
